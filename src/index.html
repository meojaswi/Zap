More formats

Other formats: add codes after a colon, like {date:DD/MM/YYYY} or {time:hh:mm A}. Codes: YYYY YY MMMM MMM MM DD D dddd ddd for dates; HH (24-hour) hh (12-hour) mm ss A (AM/PM) for times. Put other words in [brackets]. Dates from today: {date+7}, {date-1:DD/MM/YYYY}, with w, m or y for weeks, months or years, like {date+2w}.

    How Zap protects your data
    • Snippets are encrypted on disk with Windows DPAPI, so only your Windows account can read them.
    • Pasted text is kept out of Win+V clipboard history and cloud clipboard sync, and your previous clipboard text is put back.
    • Sensitive snippets are typed key by key and never go through the clipboard.
    • Keystrokes are never written to disk or logged.
    • Usage stats (how often each snippet is used, and the last date) stay on this PC, encrypted. You can turn them off or reset them above.
    • Zap connects to GitHub to check for updates, and once a day to our server for the anonymous user count: a random ID and the version, nothing else. You can turn both off above.
    • Updates are signed, and Zap refuses any download that isn't.
